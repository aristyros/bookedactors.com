Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Booked is made by BuyBye Inc., Ontario, Canada. This page says what the app collects, what it does with it, and what it never does.

The short version

  • Your scripts, rehearsals and self-tapes are not used to train AI. Not by us, and not by the services we use.
  • Booked never uploads your self-tapes. They stay on your phone and in your Photos library unless you share them or your iPhone backs them up.
  • A script’s text (and, for scans, the page images) goes to two services to make the reader: one to understand the script, one to voice the other characters. Neither trains on it.
  • You only need to sign in for the audition log. Everything else works without signing in.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app. It removes your account and your audition log for good.

What the app collects

Scripts

When you import a PDF, the app reads its text on your phone. That text is sent to a language model service (OpenAI) that identifies the characters and their lines. For scans, the page images go with it. The other characters’ lines are sent as text to a voice service (ElevenLabs) that records them. Both use it only to build your reader, do not train on it, and may hold it briefly for abuse checks under their own policies. The PDF file itself stays on your phone.

Rehearsals

While you rehearse, the app listens for your lines so the reader knows when to come in. That listening happens on the phone, using Apple’s on-device speech recognition. No audio of you leaves the phone during a rehearsal.

Self-tapes

Takes are recorded with the phone’s camera and microphone and saved to your Photos library, with a copy kept in the app until Photos has it. Booked never uploads them. They leave your phone only if you share one, or through your own iCloud backup and iCloud Photos.

Account and audition log

If you track your auditions, you sign in with Apple or Google. We receive the name and email address the provider gives us, or Apple’s private relay address if you hide your email. Your audition log (projects, roles, dates, outcomes and the notes you type) is stored in our database (Supabase, hosted in Canada) so it follows you across devices.

A device identity

On first launch the app creates an anonymous account: an ID and a creation date, with no name, email or content. It is what the Support ID in Settings points to, so we can find your records if you write in. Signing in with Apple or Google attaches to that same ID.

Analytics

We use Mixpanel to understand how the app is used: which screens are reached, whether an import succeeded or failed and why, whether a rehearsal was completed, and the like. These events carry counts, types and durations, and are tied to your device identity. They never contain script text, character names, project names, file names, audio or transcripts. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Advertising measurement

To learn which ads bring people to Booked, our paywall service (Superwall) may match an install to the ad that led to it and report subscriptions back to the ad network (such as Meta or Apple Search Ads). This uses no advertising identifier and does not follow you across other apps.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. We keep a record of the purchase, keyed by Apple’s transaction identifier, to provide what you paid for and to answer refund requests.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your scripts, rehearsals or self-tapes to train AI, and the services we use are barred from doing so.
  • We never upload your self-tapes.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never use an advertising identifier.

Services we rely on

  • OpenAI: understands a script so the reader knows who says what. Training is off.
  • ElevenLabs: voices the other characters’ lines. Training is off.
  • Supabase: stores accounts and audition logs, in Canada.
  • Mixpanel: product analytics, as described above.
  • Superwall: subscriptions and advertising measurement.
  • Apple: sign in, purchases, and on-device speech recognition.
  • Google: sign in, if you choose it.

Diagnostics

If the app crashes and your iPhone is set to share crash reports, Apple sends us one. It describes what the app was doing, never your script or your camera.

Where your data is processed

Accounts and audition logs are stored in Canada. OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Mixpanel and Superwall are in the United States, so script text and usage events are processed there, under this policy.

Why we are allowed to

For the EU and UK: we process your data to provide what you asked for (the reader, the takes, the log), and on our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used and which ads bring people to it. The phone’s permission prompts cover the phone’s features. You can ask us to stop the analytics or ad measurement for your device at any time.

Keeping and deleting

Scripts, takes and the reader’s audio live on your phone and go when you delete them or the app; our server keeps none of them. Your account and audition log stay until you delete your account in Settings. Deleting removes the account and the log for good; analytics events stay for up to two years under an ID that no longer links to anyone, and we delete them too if you ask. Purchase records are kept as long as the law and Apple’s refund process require.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Deleting your account from Settings does most of this. For anything else, write to us; we answer within a month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Booked is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and we hold their data, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before the change takes effect. The date at the top is the date of the current version.

Contact

Write to Aris at aris@bookedactors.com. Quoting the Support ID from Settings helps.
