Support

Booked is coming to the App Store. Write to be told the day it lands.

Booked is made by one actor, so you are writing to him. Email aris@bookedactors.com, or tap the ? on the app’s home screen and choose Message Aris, which opens an email with your Support ID already in it.

Sides by email

Open the email, tap the PDF, tap Share, and pick Booked. The first time, Booked is under More: tap More, then Edit, then the + beside Booked, then the checkmark to save. From then on it sits at the front of the sheet.

The import did not go through

Sides over 12 pages are not accepted. Scans and photos work when they are sharp and bright; a blurry or dark scan may come back with no lines found. Try a cleaner copy, or ask for the PDF.

The reader cannot hear me

Booked needs the microphone and speech recognition permissions, both asked for the first time you rehearse. If you said no, turn them on in Settings, Privacy & Security. Rehearse somewhere quiet, and keep the phone within arm’s reach.

Where are my takes?

In your Photos library, and in the takes box in the app. If Photos access was declined, takes are kept in the app only.

Deleting your account

Settings, then Delete account, at the bottom. It removes your account and audition log for good. Scripts and takes stay, since they only exist on your phone.

Your Support ID

Settings, Account, Support ID. Tap it to copy. Quote it when you write in and your records can be found without your email address.
