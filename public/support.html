Support

Email aris@bookedactors.com, or tap the ? on the app’s home screen and choose Message Aris, which opens an email with your Support ID already in it.

Sides by email

Open the email, tap the PDF, tap Share, and pick Booked. The first time, Booked is under More: tap More, then Edit, then the + beside Booked, then the checkmark to save. From then on it sits at the front of the sheet.

The import did not go through

Sides over 12 pages are not accepted. Scans and photos work when they are sharp and bright; a blurry or dark scan may come back with no lines found. Try a cleaner copy, or export it as a PDF.

The reader cannot hear me

Booked needs the microphone and speech recognition permissions, both asked for the first time you rehearse. If you said no, turn them on in Settings, Privacy & Security. Rehearse somewhere quiet, and keep the phone within arm’s reach.

Where are my takes?

In your Photos library, and in the app. To get to them in the app, open any script, tap Record, then tap the small round thumbnail at the bottom right, the same spot as in the iPhone camera. If Photos access was declined, takes are kept in the app only.

What is free

Downloading, the audition log, the demo scene, and importing up to five scripts to read on screen. Rehearsing and recording with a reader for your own scripts need a subscription. Apple’s sheet shows the price in your currency before you buy.

Cancelling a subscription

Deleting the app or your Booked account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings: tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then Booked. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep the reader until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

New phone, or the reader says you are not subscribed

Settings, then Restore Purchases. Your subscription belongs to your Apple ID, so it comes back on any phone signed in to the same one. If it still does not, write in with your Support ID.

I have a code

Tap Have a code? on the subscription screen, or in Settings. Type the code from a friend, an acting teacher, or someone you follow. The app shows the offer, then opens Apple’s sheet, where you see the real price in your currency and confirm. One introductory offer per account; Apple decides whether you qualify.

Bring a friend

Subscribers find Bring a friend in Settings, and by pressing and holding the Booked icon on the Home Screen. Share your code. Your friend gets the referral discount on their first months, and after their first paid renewal, their second month, you earn a free month.

Claiming a free month

Earned months show on the Bring a friend screen with a Claim one free month button. Tap it while your subscription is active, confirm Apple’s sheet (it shows $0), and the screen says Your next month is free. The free month is your next renewal, not the month you are in. One is applied per renewal, so if you earned several, claim the next one once the free month has begun, and they run back to back. If the button is missing, there is nothing waiting to claim, or your next month is already free.

Thinking of deleting Booked?

Press and hold the Booked icon on your Home Screen and choose Deleting? Get a free audition. Tell me what got in the way, and I add a script with its reader to your account by hand, so you can rehearse it and record it.

Deleting your account

Settings, then Delete account, at the bottom. It removes your account, your audition log, your referral code and any free months you have not claimed, for good. Scripts and takes stay, since they only exist on your phone. It does not cancel a subscription; see cancelling above.

Your Support ID

Settings, Account, Support ID. Tap it to copy. Quote it when you write in and your records can be found without your email address.
