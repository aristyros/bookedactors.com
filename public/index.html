Questions

How do I self-tape without a reader?

Use a reader app. Booked reads the other characters in a natural voice, waits while you speak, and comes in on your last word, so you can record the take alone. Casting hears a real back-and-forth, not you acting against a recording of your own voice.

How can I learn lines faster?

Run the scene out loud, with a reader, as many times as it takes. When you blank, say “line” and Booked feeds you the next few words instead of you stopping to look at the page.

What do I need?

An iPhone and the PDF of your sides. Booked reads it and works out who says what. No sign-up for that part.

Does it work with my sides as they come?

Yes. PDFs from casting or your agent, including scans with your start and end marks. A sharp scan works; a blurry photo of a page may not.

Is it free?

Downloading, tracking your auditions and the demo scene are free. Building a reader for your own sides costs real money per script, so that part is paid, and priced well under hiring a reader.

Does it learn from my scripts or my tapes?

No. Your scripts, rehearsals and self-tapes are not used to train AI, by us or by the services behind the reader. Takes never leave your phone.
